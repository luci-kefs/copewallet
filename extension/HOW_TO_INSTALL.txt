HOW TO LOAD COPE WALLET EXTENSION (Chrome / Edge)
==================================================

1. Open Chrome and go to:  chrome://extensions
2. Enable "Developer mode" (toggle, top-right corner)
3. Click "Load unpacked"
4. Select this folder:  C:\tmp\copewallet\extension\
5. The Cope Wallet extension icon will appear in your toolbar.

FIRST USE — ATTACH YOUR WALLET
-------------------------------
1. Click the extension icon → "Open Cope Wallet" → copewallet.com opens
2. The site will show a purple banner: "Cope Wallet Extension detected"
3. Click "Connect Extension" — enter a PIN (min 6 chars, remember it!)
4. Extension stores your wallet encrypted — it now works independently

AFTER ATTACHMENT
----------------
- Click the extension icon to open the popup
- Enter your PIN to unlock
- Send ETH, copy your address, approve dApp requests — all from the popup
- Any dApp (Uniswap, Aave, etc.) will detect window.ethereum automatically
